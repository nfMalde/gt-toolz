## Important fixes

- **The crew talks again.** In races the radio could stay silent: no radio check, no positions, no fuel.
- **The reference you chose stays.** It could be dropped after 30 seconds, and the app began to learn a new one.
- **The turn-in call comes through.** "Right, sharp" was lost in most corners. It now comes right after "now".

## New

- **More gears.** With the corner plan on, the coach calls the gears where the reference shifts in the corner, for example first gear at the apex of a hairpin.

Thanks for your feedback!
