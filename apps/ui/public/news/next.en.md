## Important fixes

- **The crew talks again.** In races the radio could stay silent: no radio check, no positions, no fuel.
- **The reference you chose stays.** It could be dropped after 30 seconds, and the app began to learn a new one.
- **The turn-in always comes.** "Right, sharp" was lost in most corners. It now comes right after "now, brake", so you know when to steer, in tight corners as the direction alone: "now, brake, right".
- **The gear in every corner where you have to shift.** It was missing in corners close together and wherever time was short. Now you hear "In two out three" or "20 percent in second gear" before the countdown, or the gear right after "now, brake".
- **Tyres up to temperature: once.** The crew said it every 5 minutes.

## New

- **The countdown names the first action.** "Two, one, now, brake". Where the reference turns in before it brakes, it runs to the turn-in: "now, right, sharp", then "brake".
- **Light braking is called.** Where the reference only brakes lightly, you hear "now, 20 percent" instead of "now, brake".
- **Your position at the turn-in.** From 2 meters beside the reference, the coach says so after the corner and on the next approach: "Start steering from the left curb."
- **Patience for coach and crew.** Under Settings → Personality you set for each how fast they get annoyed.
- **More gears.** With the corner plan on, the coach calls the gears where the reference shifts in the corner, for example first gear at the apex of a hairpin.
