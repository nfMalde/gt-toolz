GT Toolz is a free hobby project. Please read this page before you use it online. You decide how you use it, and you are responsible for that decision.

This page is not legal advice.

## What GT Toolz does

- It reads the telemetry GT7 sends on your home network. GT7 sends it encrypted, and GT Toolz decrypts it on your computer.
- It sends the PS5 two things only: the search for the console on your home network, and the request for the telemetry.
- Coach and crew talk to you on your computer's audio, overlays show your values in OBS, and a vibration device can pulse.

## What GT Toolz does not do

- It does not touch the PS5's memory, GT7's files or your save data.
- It changes nothing in the game or on the console.
- It sends no input to the game. You steer, brake and shift yourself.
- It knows nothing about other cars except your position and how many cars there are, because the telemetry has no more than that.

## Is it allowed?

We cannot promise that it is. Polyphony Digital has not published this telemetry interface, and neither Sony nor Polyphony Digital has approved GT Toolz.

**Online races and events.** The calls are a real-time aid that other drivers may not have. Leagues, tournaments and other organisers may forbid such aids, also when you practise in their lobbies, and they may penalise or exclude you. Check the rules before you join. If they say nothing, ask the organiser.

**Your PlayStation account.** The [PlayStation Terms of Service](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/legal/psn-terms-of-service/) apply to you, among them the rules on software and encryption, and Sony can suspend an account that breaks them. We cannot tell you how they apply to tools like this one. Read the version for your country.

**Game updates.** A game or system update can change or end the telemetry. GT Toolz would then stop working, possibly for good.

## Switching things off

| You want | Do this |
|---|---|
| No braking calls in races | Switch off **Crew → Settings → Corner calls in races** |
| No radio in races | Switch off the crew under [Modules](settings.md#modules) |
| No coach | Switch off the coach under [Modules](settings.md#modules) |
| Nothing at all during an event | Close GT Toolz |
