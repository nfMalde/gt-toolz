# Code of conduct

GT Toolz is made by people who like racing games, for people who like racing games. Everyone is welcome here,
whatever their experience, background or lap times.

- **Be kind.** Disagree with ideas, never attack people. No insults, harassment, discrimination or sexual content.
- **Be constructive.** Explain what you mean and why; assume good intentions; help newcomers find their way.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post other people's personal data, and no player names from logs or recordings without
  their consent.
- **Keep it on topic.** Issues and pull requests are about GT Toolz.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and block people who do not keep to this. If something
bothers you, write to the maintainer through a [private security report](https://github.com/nfMalde/gt-toolz/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message on one of the channels in the README; it stays confidential.
